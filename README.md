# What’s in the Bottle? — CBM survey companion

This website is the public companion to **“What’s in the Bottle? A Survey and Roadmap of Concept Bottleneck Models.”** It gives an accessible overview of how Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs) work, how existing CBM research can be categorized, and which challenges remain open.

## What the website covers

- The CBM framework: `x → c → y`, where an input is transformed into human-understandable concepts before a final prediction or generation step.
- The CBM taxonomy: input, concept, output, and training modules, with their main design choices.
- A literature matrix of named primary CBM research contributions, organized by modality, concept source, grounding, activations, predictor, task, and training strategy.
- Research developments from the original image-classification setting toward language, video, tabular, time-series, graph, medical, generative, and multimodal systems.
- Challenges and open problems involving concept quality, grounding, completeness, leakage, interventions, faithfulness, and human usefulness.
- A suggestion form for adding a missing CBM work or correcting a taxonomy classification.

The literature matrix is an orientation layer for browsing the field. Surveys, roadmaps, broad context papers, and umbrella placeholders are excluded from the primary-work list.
